Checkable sentences, weakest first

This reads structure, not truth. It cannot tell you whether a claim is factually correct — only whether the text is written like reliable reporting or like a forward. A high score is not a fact-check, and a low one is not proof of a lie.

The link model is a direct port of the scoring documented in DeepShield's docs/SCORING.md: score = 100 − clamp(35 + Σ weights, 0, 100). The same evidence always produces the same number, and every point is shown.
